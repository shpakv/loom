Use the `loom-spike-method` skill (read `loom-core` first).

Question from the user: $ARGUMENTS

Start by sharpening the question until it is falsifiable, then agree the
time-box, the kill criterion, the fidelity axis, the cheapest rung that can
falsify it, and the `Faked:` list — all before anything is built. The probe's
code is throwaway and gets no tests, review or conventions; keep it quarantined.
When results come back, separate raw numbers from interpretation, and say which
results ran through a fake.
