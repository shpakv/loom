Contracts first, tracks as conflict-free workstreams, tasks sized to one
session. `## Spec` is the whole task file: behavior, acceptance, contracts and
out of scope, without prescribing implementation internals. Finish with review
of `design.md`; the approved epic and task specs are the durable result.
